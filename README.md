## 👷‍♂️ About Me

I'm a BEng(Hons) Electronic & Electrical Engineering graduate from the University of Huddersfield and EngTech from the Institution of Engineering and Technology. I completed my Industrial Placement at Sellafield Ltd as a Control & Electrical Design Engineer on the Electrical Distribution Network Upgrade Project, working on high voltage cable calculations, developing protection single line diagrams , and cyber security justification for protection relays, developing risk reduction tools and designing software delivery lifecycle for electronic systems.
